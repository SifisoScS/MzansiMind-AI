# MzanziMind AI

![MzanziMind AI master roadmap: phases 0–12 from foundation through Pan-African expansion, with the 2027–2035 vision](branding/Roadmap_Overview.png)

## Vision

To build Africa's first AI-native learning and empowerment platform that transforms millions of people into highly skilled, financially intelligent, innovative citizens.

## Mission

Provide every South African with a personalized AI mentor capable of teaching, coaching, guiding and empowering them.

## Core Pillars

- Education
- Financial Literacy
- Entrepreneurship
- Career Development
- Leadership
- Innovation

## Target Audience

- Students

- Entrepreneurs
- Professionals
- Rural Communities
- Job Seekers
